# 01 — Data

Generated by `make data` (`python -m pose_estimation.experiments.data_stats`) from the prepared cache.

* Dataset: **urfall-cam0** (UR Fall Detection, University of Rzeszów, official cam0 RGB frames + official per-frame labels).
* Videos: **70** = 30 fall + 40 ADL; independent groups: 70.
* Subjects: not published per sequence by the dataset authors (subject-level split impossible).
* Frames: 11936 (2995 in fall videos, 8941 in ADL videos), 30 fps, 6.6 min total; frames/video min/median/max = 55/154/400.
* Frames where YOLOv8n-pose found no person (interpolated): 15.86%.
* Annotated falling interval (label 0) length, frames: min/median/max = 30/30.0/30; fall videos without a usable annotation: 0; frames absent from the label CSVs: 392.
